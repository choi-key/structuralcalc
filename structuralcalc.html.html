Kalkulator Panjang Penyaluran Tulangan
📖 Acuan: SNI 2847:2019 Pasal 25.4 - Perhitungan development length untuk tulangan tarik (ld), tekan (ldc), dan kait standar (ldh).
Rumus ld (tarik): ld = (fy × ψt × ψe × ψs × ψg) / (2,1 × λ × √fc') × db
Rumus ldc (tekan): ldc = max[0,24×fy×db/(λ×√fc'), 0,043×fy×db]
Rumus ldh (kait): ldh = (0,24×ψe×ψc×ψr×fy×db)/(λ×√fc')

Kumpulan Rumus Struktural

🧱 Beton (SNI 2847:2019)

Modulus Elastisitas: Ec = 4.700 × √fc' (MPa)
Kuat Tarik Lentur: fct = 0,62 × √fc' (MPa)
Kuat Tarik Belah: fct,sp = 1,2 × fct
Modulus Geser: Gc = Ec / [2(1 + ν)]
Panjang Penyaluran Tarik: ld = (fy × ψt × ψe × ψs × ψg) / (2,1 × λ × √fc') × db
Panjang Penyaluran Tekan: ldc = max[0,24×fy×db/(λ×√fc'), 0,043×fy×db]
Panjang Kait Standar: ldh = (0,24×ψe×ψc×ψr×fy×db)/(λ×√fc')

⚙️ Baja (SNI 1729:2020)

Berat WF: [(H×t1) + 2×(B×t2) - 2×(t1×t2)] × 0,00785 kg/m
Berat Pelat: Tebal(mm) × 7,85 kg/m²
Berat Pipa: (OD - t) × t × 0,02466 kg/m
Momen Inersia Persegi: I = bh³/12
Modulus Penampang: S = I/c

📐 Balok (Mekanika Struktur)

Sederhana + UDL: M = wL²/8 | δ = 5wL⁴/(384EI)
Sederhana + Point: M = PL/4 | δ = PL³/(48EI)
Kantilever + UDL: M = wL²/2 | δ = wL⁴/(8EI)
Kantilever + Point: M = PL | δ = PL³/(3EI)
Jepit-Jepit + UDL: M = wL²/12 | δ = wL⁴/(384EI)
Jepit-Jepit + Point: M = PL/8 | δ = PL³/(192EI)
